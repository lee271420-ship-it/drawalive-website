## 2. Information we process

We follow data-minimisation principles and process information that is reasonably necessary to provide, secure, and improve the Service.

### A. Content you choose to use

When you use a creation feature, we may process:

- photographs, scans, drawings, worksheets, or other images you select or capture;
- generated images, generated videos, and temporary processing artifacts;
- creation settings such as selected style, motion, aspect ratio, and generation status; and
- information you include in a support request.

You control what you choose to submit. Please do not upload a photograph, a child’s image, a name, school information, medical information, or another person’s personal information unless you have the right and appropriate permission to do so.

### B. Device, app, and service information

The MVP does not require an account, username, password, or social login. The app creates a random anonymous device identifier and sends it with requests so that the Service can maintain generation status, usage allowances, subscription entitlements, prevent abuse, and return results to the correct app installation.

Depending on the request and the platform, we may also process:

- app version, operating-system version, device model, language, and time zone;
- request timestamps, feature selections, job identifiers, status, latency, and error information;
- approximate network and security information that infrastructure providers ordinarily receive when handling a request, such as IP address and request headers; and
- diagnostic information needed to investigate crashes, abuse, fraud, or service failures.

We do not use the anonymous identifier to create a public profile, and we do not use the Service for advertising-based profiling.

### C. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are made through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payment information under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy; DrawAlive does not receive your full payment-card number.

To activate and maintain a subscription, we and our subscription infrastructure may receive or process product identifiers, transaction or entitlement status, purchase and renewal dates, expiration or cancellation status, refund status, and an app/device identifier. RevenueCat may process this information as our subscription-management service provider. We use it to provide the benefits displayed in the app, including:

- **Monthly plan:** 100 image generations and 5 video generations per subscription period; and
- **Annual plan:** 1,200 image generations and 60 video generations per annual subscription period.

The exact price, taxes, renewal terms, and available products are shown by Apple at purchase.

## 3. How we use information

We use information for the following purposes:

1. **Provide the requested creation.** We send an image to the generation pipeline when you ask DrawAlive to validate, transform, or animate it, then return the result or a processing status.
2. **Operate the Service.** We maintain anonymous job state, enforce generation allowances, restore purchases, display local history, and respond to support requests.
3. **Protect users and the Service.** We detect abuse, fraud, security incidents, unlawful use, and technical attacks; enforce the User Agreement; and keep the Service reliable.
4. **Improve reliability.** We use aggregated or operational information to diagnose failures, measure performance, and improve the user experience. We do not use your private drawings to train a public generative model unless we first obtain a separate permission that clearly explains that use.
5. **Meet legal obligations.** We retain or disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, valid legal process, or the rights and safety of users, the public, and DrawAlive.

## 5. Where processing happens and who receives information

We share information only as needed for the purposes described in this Policy. Categories of recipients include:

- **Apple:** App Store billing, transaction processing, subscription management, and device permissions;
- **RevenueCat:** subscription and entitlement management;
- **Cloudflare:** edge hosting, request handling, security, Durable Objects, and optional database infrastructure for anonymous usage and subscription metadata;
- **Generation providers:** image and video processing providers selected for the requested feature. The current deployment may use OpenAI for image generation and PixStag or another configured provider for video generation. These providers receive the media and request parameters needed to perform the requested generation and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices;
- **Support and professional advisers:** only when needed to answer a request, protect rights, or comply with law; and
- **Authorities or other parties:** when required by law, legal process, or a good-faith belief that disclosure is necessary to protect people, property, or the Service.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs in the MVP.

## 6. Media storage and retention

DrawAlive is designed to minimise server-side media retention:

- Images and creations saved in the app are stored in the app’s local Application Support storage on your device.
- A compressed image is sent to the generation pipeline only when you request validation, image generation, or video generation.
- The Cloudflare Worker coordinates a job and forwards the request to a generation provider. The Worker is configured not to keep a permanent library of your original images, generated images, or videos.
- Temporary job state, signed result references, logs, usage records, and subscription metadata may remain for as long as reasonably needed to complete the request, prevent abuse, reconcile credits, resolve disputes, comply with law, and maintain security.
- External providers may have their own retention periods and policies. We cannot control or promise the retention practices of a third-party provider; review its privacy notice before using the corresponding feature.

You can delete creations saved on the device from DrawAlive Settings. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local storage under the operating system’s rules. Deleting local media does not automatically delete records already required for billing, security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.
